Escort in Cyprus: what to expect

Northern Cyprus is small and the distances between Kyrenia, Nicosia, Famagusta, Iskele and Guzelyurt are short — but the difference between a hotel and a private flat is not. Meeting in the lobby is the usual route; going straight up to the room without leaving a name at reception is also possible. At gated complexes and flats, if a name has to be given at the barrier, you tell us in advance and we pass it on.

On price there is no haggling: the tariff is published and does not change at the door. The 30 minute rate is in-call only, because travel time eats into an outcall. Hotel and home visits start at the 1 hour rate; people planning a night out take 4 hours.

What changes by area

Central Kyrenia, the harbour and Zeytinlik are walkable and therefore the easiest place to meet. Alsancak, Lapta, Catalkoy and Esentepe are hillside and coastal villas — without a location pin time is lost at the gate. In Famagusta the Sakarya and Maras side works separately, in Iskele the Long Beach and Bafra resort strip, in Guzelyurt the Lefke direction. In Nicosia, Dereboyu and Koskluciftlik are the first choice for business travellers.

When to write

In the centre 10-15 minutes notice is enough. At distant points and resort strips write 1-2 hours ahead; last minute does not land there. Casino exits and late requests cluster between 22:00 and 02:00, and 10-15 minutes is enough in that window.
