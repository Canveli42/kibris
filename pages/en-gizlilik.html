Privacy policy

This page explains in plain language what data is processed when you visit RedKıbrıs, where it is stored, and what rights you have. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Short summary

RedKıbrıs is a showcase and referral site. There is no membership, no cart and no payment step. The amount of data we collect is therefore deliberately kept very small.

If you only browse the site, we do not ask for your name, phone or e-mail. Data only comes into play when you message us on WhatsApp, Telegram or by phone.

Data processed

While you browse, the hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, visit time and requested page.

When you write to us, the name, phone, area, date-time and notes you give over WhatsApp or Telegram are used to carry out the booking.

Measurement and local storage

There are no cookies at all: no advertising cookies, no tracking pixels. That is why the consent banner is gone.

Visits are measured without cookies. Page views and clicks are counted using only the page path, the clicked link and the referring site. The IP address is not stored and no identifier is written to your browser. Search engine bots are filtered out and records are deleted after 30 days.

Your language preference is kept only in your own browser under the rk_lang key and is not sent to the server.

Third-party links

WhatsApp, Telegram and phone links take you to other platforms. Those platforms' own privacy rules apply there.

Hosting and infrastructure

The site is hosted on Vercel and content and images are served from Supabase. There is no payment infrastructure and no user account; because payment is in cash, no card details pass through the site.

Retention

  • Messages: a reasonable period after the booking, longer for repeat clients so they are recognised easily.
  • Server logs: for the provider's standard retention period.
  • Measurement data: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Local keys: until you delete them, on your own device only.

Your rights

Under KVKK you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to learn the purpose, to ask for correction and deletion, and to object to processing. A WhatsApp message or e-mail is enough to make a request.

Children

The site is aimed at users aged 18 and over.

Changes

If the policy is updated, the date on this page changes.
