About

RedKıbrıs is an independent escort guide based in central Kyrenia. We cover the island by area, with fixed published rates and no deposit.

In one line: prices on the page, nothing paid in advance, payment taken before you go in, replies in three languages.

How we work

  • Rates are fixed. Published on the page, no haggling, no change at the door.
  • No deposit. Nothing upfront, no card and no transfer in advance.
  • Payment before you go in. Taken at the start of the meeting, before the room or the flat; cash, transfer or USDT.
  • Taxi is transparent. Outside central Kyrenia the fare is calculated by distance and always quoted first.
  • A short message is enough. Send area, time and duration. No forms.

Where we are

Our base is central Kyrenia. Most work runs through the centre, the harbour area, Zeytinlik and Karaoglanoglu; we also travel to Alsancak, Lapta, Catalkoy, Bellapais and Esentepe. Nicosia, Famagusta, Iskele and Guzelyurt have their own pages.

The 30 minute rate is in-call only, at our place. An outcall starts at 1 hour.

How to reach us

WhatsApp, Telegram or phone. There is a real person on the line, day and night. We answer in Turkish, English and Russian.

What we are not

  • Not a call centre. You write to someone who knows the work, not a script.
  • Not a huge directory. Instead of hundreds of listings we send the current selection for your area.
  • Not one of those deposit sites. The most common complaint is the deposit; we do not take one.

One limit to know

Availability moves with the day and the hour. 22:00-02:00 is the busiest window and distant points need 30-60 minutes notice. Last minute does not always leave room.
