How the tariff works
A 30-minute meeting is only at our place; for a visit to your home or hotel the 1-hour tariff applies as a minimum. Longer bookings are agreed in advance.
Taxi fare
The fare rises with the distance to the meeting point. In central Kyrenia it is usually not charged; in Iskele, Guzelyurt and Lefke it is calculated by distance.
Payment
Cash, IBAN / bank transfer or crypto (USDT). No prepayment — payment is made during the meeting.
